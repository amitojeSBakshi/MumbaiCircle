Venues to follow directly

  • NCPA, Nariman PointIndia's premier performing-arts centre. Its genre archive lists individually dated shows, including several genuine weeknight ones
  • G5A Foundation, WorliContemporary arts and performance. Runs genuinely dark some weeks — check rather than assume
  • Alliance Française de BombayFilm club and the French cultural programme — "Rendezvous with French Cinema" runs through August
  • Royal Opera House, GirgaonRestored 1911 opera house, now a proper touring venue for musicals and drama
  • Prithvi Theatre, JuhuThe city's best-known intimate stage. Its own site carries no browsable schedule — check BookMyShow or call the box office directly

Every card carries the nearest railway line and station — the suburban network, not the Metro, since the local is how most of this city actually moves. That is the closest station to the venue's area rather than a door-to-door route, and Powai in particular has no station of its own: the nearest ones still mean an auto at the end. Confirm price and timing on the linked page before you travel; small venues change schedules constantly. Where a night is listed as recurring without a date, it is a standing weekly slot — check the venue before setting off.
